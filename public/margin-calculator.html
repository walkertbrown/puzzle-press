Which side: —
Bleed: the PDF page is 0.125" wider and 0.25" taller than the trim, and content that should bleed must extend all the way to the page edge. Margins above are measured from the PDF page edge, not the trim line.
The two mistakes that get interiors rejected. Putting the gutter on the same side of every page — it must mirror, left on odd pages and right on even. And turning on bleed without enlarging the page: with bleed the PDF must be 0.125" wider and 0.25" taller than the trim size, or KDP will reject it for the wrong page size.

Page size with bleed, for every trim

Bleed is for artwork that runs off the edge of the page. KDP trims 0.125" from the top, bottom and outside edge, so with bleed on, the PDF page is 0.125" wider and 0.25" taller than the trim. It is not wider by 0.25": the inside edge goes into the binding and is never trimmed.

That is worth checking, because some bleed calculators for KDP interiors give a 6 × 9 book a page of 6.25 × 9.25 (1875 pixels wide). That applies the cover's four-sided bleed to an interior, and makes every page 0.125" wider than KDP expects. KDP's own trim and bleed page gives 6.125 × 9.25 for the same book.

Pixel sizes are rounded up. Anything that should bleed must reach the page edge, and anything that must not be cut off stays inside the outside margin. With bleed, that is 0.5" from the PDF page edge (0.375" plus the 0.125" that is trimmed away), and the calculator above measures it the same way. The cover bleeds 0.125" on every side as well, and its width also depends on the spine. The spine calculator gives the full cover size.

Setting it up in Word, Google Docs or InDesign

Set the page size to the trim (or the trim plus bleed, if you use it). Turn on mirror margins (Word: Layout → Margins → Custom → Multiple pages: Mirror margins; InDesign: Facing pages). Then "inside" is the gutter figure above and "outside" is the outer figure. Export to PDF with fonts embedded — an un-embedded font is the other common rejection, and the previewer's message about it is not obvious.

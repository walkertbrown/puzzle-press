The rates

Printing cost = fixed cost + (page count × per-page cost), except for short black-ink books, which are a flat rate. Large trim means more than 6.12" wide or more than 9" tall — so 6×9 is regular trim, but 7×10, 8×10 and 8.5×11 are not.

Will the cover print?

Page count decides the spine too, and a spine that is even a little wrong gets the wrap rejected. The spine calculator gives the exact cover size for the same book — and flags the 0.06" that several popular calculators add and Amazon's documentation does not.

Why puzzle books price the way they do

A puzzle book is black ink, so printing is cheap: a 120-page 6×9 costs $2.44 to print. The trap is page count. Every extra puzzle adds a page and a bit over a penny, and solutions pages add more — so a 200-puzzle book is not twice the margin of a 100-puzzle one, it is twice the print cost with the same list price.

The other trap is large trim. Going from 6×9 to 8.5×11 for bigger grids raises the per-page cost from $0.012 to $0.017 — about 40% more per page. Worth it for a large-print book people will pay more for; expensive by accident otherwise.

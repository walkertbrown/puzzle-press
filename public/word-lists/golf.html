Crossword clues for every word

The same list with a plain-language clue for each word — written for the crossword type, and free to use in a grid you build yourself. Copy the lines as they are (word — clue) into the generator's own-list box and it makes the crossword for you.

  • Ace — Hole-in-one, scored with a single perfect shot
  • Approach — Shot aimed at landing the ball on the green
  • Backswing — Motion of raising the club before striking the ball
  • Birdie — Score of one stroke under par on a hole
  • Bogey — Score of one stroke over par on a hole
  • Bunker — Sand-filled hazard that traps an errant shot
  • Caddie — Person who carries clubs and gives advice on the course
  • Chip — Short shot that lofts the ball briefly then rolls
  • Clubhouse — Building at a course with a pro shop and lounge
  • Course — Full layout of holes played during a round
  • Dogleg — Hole that bends left or right partway down the fairway
  • Draw — Shot that curves gently opposite a slice
  • Driver — Person behind the wheel
  • Eagle — Great bird of prey, national emblem
  • Eighteenhole — Standard full-length round of golf played in one outing
  • Fade — Shot that curves gently in the same direction as a slice
  • Fairway — Mowed strip of grass between tee and green
  • Flagstick — Pole topped with fabric marking the hole's location
  • Followthrough — Continued motion of the club after striking the ball
  • Fringe — Short grass bordering the putting green
  • Golf — Game of clubs, holes and greens
  • Green — Smooth putting surface around the hole
  • Handicap — Number reflecting a golfer's average skill level
  • Hook — Barbed curve on the line
  • Iron — Metal-headed club used for precise approach shots
  • Links — Seaside course built on sandy, grassy terrain
  • Masters — Prestigious spring tournament held each year in Augusta
  • Mulligan — Informal do-over shot not counted on the paper tally
  • Ninehole — Short round covering half of a full-length round
  • Par — Standard number of strokes set for a hole
  • Pin — Flagged marker showing where the hole is
  • Putt — Short stroke rolled along the green toward the cup
  • Putter — Club used for short, rolling shots on the green
  • Rough — Long uncut grass bordering the fairway
  • Sandtrap — Low area filled with fine grit that traps a ball
  • Scorecard — Paper used to track strokes for every hole played
  • Slice — Shot that curves sharply off target for a right-hander
  • Swing — Full motion used to strike the ball with a club
  • Tee — Small peg that holds the ball for the first shot
  • Tour — Series of professional events played over a season
  • Wedge — Club with a high loft used for short approach shots
  • Wood — Long club traditionally used for long-distance shots

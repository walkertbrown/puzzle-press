Crossword clues for every word

The same list with a plain-language clue for each word — written for the crossword type, and free to use in a grid you build yourself. Copy the lines as they are (word — clue) into the generator's own-list box and it makes the crossword for you.

  • Barrel — Wooden cask used to age wine before bottling
  • Bordeaux — French wine region famous for cabernet and merlot blends
  • Bottle — Holds the milk
  • Bouquet — Bunch of flowers
  • Burgundy — French wine region known for pinot noir and chardonnay
  • Cabernet — Bold red grape variety often paired with steak
  • Cellar — Cool underground room used to store wine bottles
  • Champagne — Sparkling wine for the toast
  • Chardonnay — Popular white grape variety, sometimes aged in oak
  • Chianti — Italian red wine traditionally from the Tuscany region
  • Cluster — Bunch of grapes still attached to their stems
  • Cork — Stopper pulled from a bottle before pouring
  • Corkscrew — Spiral tool used to pull a stopper from a bottle
  • Decanter — Glass vessel wine is poured into to breathe
  • Fermentation — Process where yeast turns grape sugar into alcohol
  • Finish — Lingering flavor a wine leaves after swallowing
  • Glass — Stemmed vessel wine is served and sipped from
  • Grape — Fruit that becomes wine
  • Grapevine — Woody climbing plant that produces clusters for wine
  • Harvest — Gathering in the crops
  • Label — Printed paper glued to a bottle naming its contents
  • Merlot — Soft red grape variety often blended with cabernet
  • Napa — California valley famous for wineries
  • Oak — Sturdy tree whose fruit is the acorn
  • Palate — A taster's sense of flavor on the tongue
  • Pinot — French grape name meaning pinecone, as in noir or grigio
  • Riesling — Aromatic white grape variety often sweet or crisp
  • Rioja — Spanish wine region known for tempranillo grapes
  • Sauvignon — Crisp white grape variety often paired with blanc
  • Sommelier — Trained expert who recommends and serves wine
  • Sparkling — Style of wine with bubbles from added carbon dioxide
  • Swirl — Gentle circular motion used to release a wine's aroma
  • Syrah — Bold dark-skinned grape variety, also called shiraz
  • Tannin — Compound that gives red wine its dry, puckering feel
  • Tasting — Event where several wines are sampled and compared
  • Terroir — Combination of soil and climate that shapes a wine's flavor
  • Tuscany — Italian wine region known for chianti
  • Vineyard — Land planted with rows of grapevines for wine
  • Vintage — Year in which a wine's grapes were harvested
  • Wine — Fermented drink made from crushed grapes
  • Yeast — Microorganism that converts sugar into alcohol
  • Zinfandel — Grape variety behind both bold reds and blush pinks

Crossword clues for every word

The same list with a plain-language clue for each word — written for the crossword type, and free to use in a grid you build yourself. Copy the lines as they are (word — clue) into the generator's own-list box and it makes the crossword for you.

  • Axle — Rod the wheels turn on
  • Backhaul — Paid cargo carried on the return trip
  • Bunk — Bed built into a truck's sleeper cab
  • Caravan — Group of vehicles traveling together
  • Cargo — Goods being transported
  • Checkpoint — Roadside stop where documents are inspected
  • Citation — Official ticket for breaking a rule
  • Clutch — Pedal pressed to change gear
  • Convoy — Line of trucks traveling as a group
  • Cruise — Holiday aboard a ship
  • Dashboard — Panel of dials facing the driver
  • Diesel — Heavier fuel for trucks
  • Dispatch — Office that assigns loads to drivers
  • Dockworker — Person who loads and unloads ships and trucks
  • Downshift — Drop into a lower gear
  • Driver — Person behind the wheel
  • Flatbed — Trailer with no sides or roof
  • Forklift — Machine that raises heavy pallets
  • Freight — Goods carried by a commercial vehicle
  • Fuel — Diesel or gas that powers the engine
  • Gauge — Dial that shows a reading like fuel level
  • Gearshift — Lever used to change speed settings
  • Hauler — Vehicle built to move heavy loads
  • Highway — Major road between cities
  • Hitch — Coupling that connects a trailer to a truck
  • Horn — Device sounded as a warning
  • Idling — Running an engine while parked
  • Inspection — Check of a vehicle's safety and condition
  • Interstate — Major highway that crosses several borders
  • Jackknife — Dangerous fold of trailer against cab
  • Ledger — Written log of deliveries and expenses
  • Load — Goods packed onto a vehicle
  • Logbook — Record of hours driven and rested
  • Mileage — Distance a car has travelled
  • Mirror — Glass for the view behind
  • Mudflap — Rubber guard behind a wheel
  • Odometer — Dial that counts the miles
  • Overpass — Bridge carrying one road above another
  • Pallet — Wooden platform goods are stacked on
  • Permit — Document allowing a vehicle on the road
  • Radio — Device used to talk to dispatch
  • Rearview — Mirror that shows what's behind
  • Rig — Slang for a large tractor-trailer
  • Route — Planned path from pickup to delivery
  • Sleeper — Footed pyjama suit
  • Speedometer — Gauge that shows how fast you're going
  • Tanker — Vehicle built to carry liquid cargo
  • Tarp — Waterproof cover strapped over a load
  • Terminal — Depot where freight is transferred
  • Throttle — Accelerator
  • Tire — Rubber wheel covering that grips the road
  • Toll — Fee charged to use certain roads
  • Trailer — Unpowered unit towed behind the cab
  • Transmission — Gears between engine and wheels
  • Tunnel — Passage through a hill
  • Warehouse — Large building where goods are stored
  • Weigh — Check a vehicle's load against the legal limit
  • Windshield — Glass panel a driver looks through

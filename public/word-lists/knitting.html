Crossword clues for every word

The same list with a plain-language clue for each word — written for the crossword type, and free to use in a grid you build yourself. Copy the lines as they are (word — clue) into the generator's own-list box and it makes the crossword for you.

  • Afghan — Large knitted or crocheted blanket for a couch
  • Alpaca — South American animal prized for its soft fleece
  • Beanie — Snug knitted cap worn on the head
  • Bindoff — Final row of stitches that finishes a knitted edge
  • Blanket — Warm covering
  • Cable — Twisted rope-like pattern made by crossing stitches
  • Cashmere — Luxury fiber combed from a goat's soft undercoat
  • Caston — First row of loops that starts a knitting project
  • Chart — Grid of symbols showing a colorwork design
  • Circular — Needle joined in a loop for knitting in rounds
  • Colorwork — Technique using multiple yarn shades in one project
  • Decrease — Technique that removes a stitch to narrow the fabric
  • Doublepointed — Short needle used in sets of four or five for small tubes
  • Fairisle — Colorwork style named for a small Scottish location
  • Frogging — Unraveling mistaken stitches to redo a section
  • Garter — Band tossed to the single men
  • Gauge — Dial that shows a reading like fuel level
  • Increase — Technique that adds an extra stitch to widen the fabric
  • Intarsia — Colorwork technique that knits each color block separately
  • Knit — Fabric made by looping yarn with two needles
  • Knitting — Craft of looping yarn into fabric by hand
  • Mitten — Hand covering with one section for the thumb only
  • Needle — Sharp point used for an injection
  • Pattern — Written instructions for making a specific knitted item
  • Purl — Basic stitch that creates a bumpy textured row
  • Ribbing — Stretchy stitch pattern used for cuffs and hems
  • Row — One complete pass of stitches across the work
  • Scarf — Wrapped around the neck in winter
  • Shawl — Wide piece of fabric draped over the shoulders
  • Skein — Loosely coiled bundle of yarn ready for winding
  • Slipstitch — Technique of passing a loop to the other needle without working it
  • Sock — Knitted covering worn on the foot inside a shoe
  • Stitch — Single loop of yarn or thread pulled through fabric
  • Stockinette — Smooth knit fabric with a bumpy purl side underneath
  • Swatch — Small test square knitted to check gauge
  • Sweater — Knitted top for a cold day
  • Tension — Tightness of yarn as it's pulled through the fingers
  • Wool — Soft fiber sheared from a sheep
  • Yarn — Spun fiber wound into a ball for knitting or crochet
  • Yarnover — Technique that wraps the working strand around the needle to add a stitch

Crossword clues for every word

The same list with a plain-language clue for each word — written for the crossword type, and free to use in a grid you build yourself. Copy the lines as they are (word — clue) into the generator's own-list box and it makes the crossword for you.

  • Accelerate — Speed up
  • Airbag — Cushion that inflates in a crash
  • Alternator — Charges the battery while driving
  • Axle — Rod the wheels turn on
  • Battery — Stores the electrical charge
  • Brakes — They slow you down
  • Bumper — Bar at the front and rear
  • Cabin — Small wooden house, or the inside of a car
  • Carburetor — Mixes fuel with air in old engines
  • Chassis — Frame of the vehicle
  • Clutch — Pedal pressed to change gear
  • Convertible — Car with a folding roof
  • Coolant — Liquid that stops overheating
  • Dashboard — Panel of dials facing the driver
  • Detour — Route around a closed road
  • Diesel — Heavier fuel for trucks
  • Engine — What is under the hood
  • Exhaust — Pipe for waste gases
  • Fender — Panel over the wheel, American style
  • Freeway — Fast multi-lane road
  • Garage — Where the car sleeps
  • Gasoline — Petrol
  • Gearbox — Transmission housing
  • Headlight — Front lamp
  • Highway — Major road between cities
  • Hubcap — Cover on the wheel centre
  • Ignition — Where the key turns
  • Junction — Where roads meet
  • Lanes — Marked strips of a road
  • Mileage — Distance a car has travelled
  • Mirror — Glass for the view behind
  • Motor — Engine
  • Muffler — Silencer on the exhaust
  • Odometer — Dial that counts the miles
  • Parking — Leaving the car in a space
  • Pistons — They pump inside the cylinders
  • Radiator — Cools the engine
  • Roadster — Two-seat open sports car
  • Seatbelt — Strap that holds you in
  • Sedan — Family car with a separate boot, saloon
  • Signal — Blinking indicator
  • Spoiler — Wing on the back of a sports car
  • Steering — Wheel that turns the car
  • Suspension — Springs that smooth the ride

  • Tires — Rubber round the wheels
  • Traffic — Cars on the road, especially too many
  • Transmission — Gears between engine and wheels
  • Trunk — Boot, in America
  • Wipers — Blades that clear the windscreen
